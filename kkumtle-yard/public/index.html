    Wiggles'
    Front Yard

    Bring the dry front yard back to life! The more Wiggles the worm eats and wanders, the richer the soil gets and the more fruit grows.

    • 🕹️Move · On a phone, put your thumb anywhere and slide (joystick). On a computer, move the mouse or use the arrow keys (WASD).
    • 🍂Eat food scraps 🍂☕🥚🍌🥕 to grow longer. The soil comes alive wherever you crawl.
    • 📜Finish quests to win items. Make the soil rich enough and you earn award certificates.
    • ⚠️When the ground shakes, the mole is coming. Run so it can't bite your head or tail!
    • 🐦Our cockatiel Butter flies in now and then. Stay out of the red landing mark, or she'll peck you and you lose points and length!
    • 🐔Our hen Mango pecks anything in front of her. Lure the mole in front of Mango and she'll chase it off! Crack her eggs for soil fertilizer, or leave them to hatch bug-eating chicks.
    • 🦗When a locust swarm chews up a tree, circle the wilted tree to heal it. On rainy days, dodge the yellow marks where lightning will strike! In other countries, local animals help or get in your way.
    • 🔥While flying, press F (or the 💩 button) to fire and use the arrow keys / WASD to move.
    • 🌍At yard level 3, SOS calls arrive from around the world. Fly there on Butter, chase off crows with 💩, and heal those lands too!
    • ⭐⚡Speed (1) · 🌀Burrow (2) · ⭐Super Wiggles (3 or Space) — as Super Wiggles you can slap the mole away!
